The problem

A freeze is a blunt tool.
Nobody built the tool to free the innocent.

Fraud engines find mules and trigger freezes. What happens next is manual, slow and often wrong: whole accounts locked over small amounts, sellers who did nothing wrong stuck for weeks, victims waiting for money that sits in someone else's account.

The Proportionality Index

Money held ÷ money provably disputed.
1.0 is fair.

A number every regulator can rank banks by. In Ravi's case:

0x

Whole-account freeze: INR 84,000 held for an INR 3,600 dispute.

0x

With FairFreeze: hold INR 3,600, release INR 80,400 the same day.
